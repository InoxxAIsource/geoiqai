## What is GeoIQ?

GeoIQ is an AI visibility platform that tells you **exactly where your brand appears in AI-generated answers** — and gives you a step-by-step plan to improve it.

Most brands are invisible to AI. When someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity to recommend a product in your category, your brand is probably not mentioned. GeoIQ finds those gaps and fixes them.

**Free audit at [geoiqai.com](https://geoiqai.com) — no signup needed.**

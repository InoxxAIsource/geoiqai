# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages on geoiqai.com
- Blog posts and long-form GEO education pages
- Comparison and glossary style landing pages
- Crawlability files and public metadata

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard routes and dashboard subviews
- Admin routes
- Login, signup, password reset, and verification flows, except where route handling affects public crawlability or shared metadata behavior

## Target audience
- Founders, growth leads, and marketing teams at startups, with a strong focus on Indian startups and SaaS teams

## Primary keywords
- AI visibility
- generative engine optimization
- GEO
- ChatGPT visibility
- Gemini SEO
- Perplexity SEO
- llms.txt
- AI visibility score

## Notes
- Deployment is split between a React + Vite frontend artifact and an Express API artifact.
- Public SEO checks must follow the production route owner, not just the frontend route file.

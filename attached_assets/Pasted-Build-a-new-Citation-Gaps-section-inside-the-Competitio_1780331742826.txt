Build a new "Citation Gaps" section 
inside the Competition tab or as a 
new tab called "Gaps".

This is the most requested feature —
showing users exactly which questions 
their competitors get cited in 
but they do not.

WHAT IT DOES:

Takes all tracked prompts for the domain.
For each prompt checks:
- Does your brand appear? Yes/No
- Does Competitor A appear? Yes/No
- Does Competitor B appear? Yes/No

Then shows three views:

VIEW 1 — Your gaps
Questions where competitor appears 
but you do not.
These are your highest priority 
content opportunities.

VIEW 2 — Your wins
Questions where you appear but 
competitor does not.
These are your competitive advantages.

VIEW 3 — Both missing
Questions where neither brand appears.
These are untapped opportunities 
to own the space.

UI FOR EACH GAP ROW:

Show the prompt question text
Show which AI systems the competitor 
appears in for this prompt
Show a "Create content" button that 
opens GEO Agent with this prompt 
pre-loaded as context

Example gap row:

"What are the best AI visibility 
tools for SaaS founders?"

Competitor A: ChatGPT ✅ Gemini ✅ Perplexity ✅
You: ChatGPT ❌ Gemini ❌ Perplexity ❌

[Create content to close this gap →]

Clicking "Create content" opens 
GEO Agent with this message pre-filled:

"I need to create content that gets 
GeoIQ cited when someone asks: 
'What are the best AI visibility 
tools for SaaS founders?'

My current score for this prompt is 0.
My competitor scores 3/3 AI systems.

Generate a content brief and 
specific copy I can publish to 
close this gap."


HOW TO GET COMPETITOR DATA:

You already have competitor prompts 
running in the Competition tab.

For each tracked prompt:

Query your prompts table for 
user's domain results.

Query your prompts table for 
competitor domain results 
(already tracked in Competition tab).

Compare the two sets of results.

WHERE prompt_domain = user_domain 
AND brand_mentioned = false
AND EXISTS (
  SELECT 1 FROM prompt_results 
  WHERE prompt_id = same_prompt
  AND prompt_domain = competitor_domain
  AND brand_mentioned = true
)

These rows are the gaps.


SUMMARY STATS at top of Gaps view:

"You are missing from X prompts 
where Competitor A appears"

"You own X prompts where 
Competitor A does not appear"

"X prompts are unclaimed by both — 
first to create content wins"

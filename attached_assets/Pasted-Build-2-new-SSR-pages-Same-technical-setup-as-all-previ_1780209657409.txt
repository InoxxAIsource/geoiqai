H2: The 7 AI Search Ranking Factors in 2026

Factor 1: Citation Velocity
How frequently and recently your brand 
has been cited by authoritative sources.
AI training data prioritizes brands with 
consistent citation history, not one-off mentions.
Score impact: +20 points
Fix: G2 reviews, Crunchbase, ProductHunt,
industry publication features

Factor 2: Entity Consistency
Same brand name, description, and category
across all platforms — Crunchbase, LinkedIn,
llms.txt, homepage, G2.
If descriptions conflict, AI systems fragment
your entity signal and reduce citation confidence.
Score impact: +15 points
Fix: One canonical 2-sentence brand description
pasted identically everywhere

Factor 3: Semantic Completeness
Content scoring 8.5/10+ on semantic completeness
is 4.2x more likely to be cited in AI Overviews.
Cover all related concepts, not just keywords.
Score impact: +15 points
Fix: Answer-first content, FAQ sections,
related concept coverage

Factor 4: Technical AI Crawler Access
GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Bingbot
must be allowed in robots.txt.
Less than 30% of websites have this correctly
configured.
Score impact: +10 points
Fix: robots.txt update (10 minutes)

Factor 5: llms.txt File
Plain text file at yourdomain.com/llms.txt
gives AI systems structured context about
your brand before parsing individual pages.
Under 5% of websites have one.
Score impact: +10 points
Fix: Create llms.txt (15 minutes)

Factor 6: Structured Data (Schema)
Organization schema, Article schema, FAQPage
schema signal structured trustworthy content
to AI extraction models.
Score impact: +10 points
Fix: Add JSON-LD schema to homepage and pages

Factor 7: Third-Party Citation Authority
Quality of sites that mention your brand.
LinkedIn is the #1 most-cited domain for
professional queries (Profound research 2026).
Crunchbase, G2, ProductHunt all heavily
indexed by AI training pipelines.
Score impact: +20 points
Fix: Submit to all major directories,
get publication features

H2: Platform-specific ranking factors

ChatGPT ranking factors:
- Training data coverage before cutoff
- Citation frequency across authoritative sources
- Brand entity consistency
- Crunchbase and G2 presence

Gemini ranking factors:
- Google knowledge graph presence
- Organization schema on homepage
- Coverage in Google-indexed publications
- YourStory, Inc42 for Indian brands

Perplexity ranking factors:
- PerplexityBot crawler access (most critical)
- Answer-first content in first 150 words
- Live web indexing via Bing
- Real-time content freshness
